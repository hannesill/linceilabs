01 Why Lincei

The agents you know, set up for clinical research.

On its own, a coding agent starts from an empty folder. It writes SQL against whatever it finds, chooses clinical definitions from memory, and reports results in a chat that scrolls away. Lincei runs the same agent with clinical data, validated definitions and a research board already in place, and tells it to work the way a study needs. You keep your subscription and your choice of agent.

02 Research board

Table 1 first. Then your call.

Agents publish to a board beside the conversation. Here the agent built a Sepsis-3 cohort from the MIMIC-IV demo, put Table 1 on a card, and posted an approval card after it. Modelling waits until you approve the cohort or narrow it.

  • T

    Provenance on every card

    The dataset, the script that made the result and the time it ran sit under each card, ready to check or rerun.

  • !

    Decisions on the record

    Approve a cohort or send it back from the card. Your answer and its time stay with the study, and the agent reads it from there.

  • S

    Studies that last

    Cards stay on disk with your project, come back in later conversations, and export as one self-contained HTML file.

03 Images

Chest X-rays go on the board, not into the model.

Agents put a set of films on a card as a gallery, with captions and tags. Open a film to zoom and pan, switch overlays such as segmentation masks on and off, and answer the agent by clicking the pictures.

  • I

    Only the films a cohort needs

    For MIMIC-CXR, Lincei asks for your PhysioNet login when an agent needs images, then downloads that cohort's films.

  • P

    Pixels stay off the model

    Agents never open credentialed images. You read the films on the board; the agent works with file names and labels.

  • !

    Answers on the pictures

    Click the films to answer a question. Your picks come back to the agent as data it can tally on a new card.

04 In the loop

The agent asks before it guesses.

Clinical definitions are full of choices: which SOFA window, which ICU stay, when the 30 days start. Lincei tells every agent to stop and ask when a choice would change the result, and to state its cohort, exclusions and denominators when it reports.

  • Questions arrive in the conversation with answers to pick from.
  • Exploratory findings are labelled exploratory.
  • Your PhysioNet login goes into Lincei's own dialog, never the chat, and is not stored.

05 Clinical data

Set up for clinical data on first launch.

Lincei installs its own Python environment with M4, Vitrine, pandas, DuckDB, lifelines and statsmodels. Every agent finds it, so no project needs its own setup.

From the open demo to full MIMIC-IV

The MIMIC-IV demo downloads during setup. Add MIMIC-IV, MIMIC-IV-Note, MIMIC-IV-ED, eICU, MIMIC-III or MIMIC-CXR when you have PhysioNet access, or a folder of your own CSV files. Your PhysioNet password is used once and never stored.

Private by default

No telemetry, no Lincei account, no hosted relay. Data stays on your computer unless an agent sends it to the model provider you picked. Settings, Clinical Data shows the exact instructions Lincei gives every agent.

Made for researchers

Terminals, diffs and git stay out of sight, and agents explain their work in plain language. Switch on Techie mode in Settings when you want the code.

06 Agents

Bring the agent you already use.

Lincei doesn't sell model access. Sign in to Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Cursor, Grok or Antigravity with your own subscription, switch between them per conversation, and Lincei runs them on your computer, next to your data.
