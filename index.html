02 Research cards

Results go on cards, not into the scroll.

Agents publish tables and charts to a research board beside the conversation, powered by Vitrine. Each card keeps its dataset, its query and the time it ran. When a step needs your sign-off, the card waits for it.

  • T

    Provenance on every card

    The dataset, query and timestamp sit under each result, ready to check or rerun.

  • D

    Decisions on the record

    Approve a cohort or send it back from the card. What you chose, and when, stays with the study.

  • C

    Studies that last

    Cards persist on disk with your project and export as one self-contained HTML file.

03 In the loop

The agent asks before it guesses.

Clinical definitions are full of choices: which SOFA window, which ICU stay, which death date. Lincei tells agents to stop and ask when a choice would change the result.

  • Questions arrive in the thread with the likely answer preselected.
  • Auto mode approves routine steps and asks before risky ones.
  • Nothing touches credentialed data until you confirm it.

04 Clinical data

Set up for clinical data on first launch.

Lincei installs its own Python environment with M4, pandas, DuckDB, lifelines and statsmodels. Every agent and terminal finds it, so no project needs its own setup.

MIMIC-IV demo, ready to query

The open MIMIC-IV demo downloads during setup. Add full MIMIC-IV, eICU or your own files later. Your PhysioNet password is used once and never stored.
